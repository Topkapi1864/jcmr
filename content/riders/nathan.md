I have many fond memories of time spent with Nanna Janet and so when the opportunity came to do something more difficult than I've ever done, in memory of her, I took no time to sign up. Never having done anything of note in endurance events this was never going to be easy, but 12 months ago it seemed like a good idea: exercise, focus, weight loss, and a proud moment for charity. The reality has been weight gain and miserable exercise with the proud moment still yet to come!
