Meet Steve: husband, proud father of three girls and professional listener, a skill finely tuned at home, where he is comprehensively outnumbered.

His family is his greatest asset and purpose. His wife and daughters are at the heart of everything he does, and being someone they can rely on, and be proud of, matters most.

Unfortunately, his commitment to being a good role model has landed him on a bike for 240 miles, alongside a team that is about as athletic as a Sunday pub quiz team. Some “used-to-be” nostalgia is being rebranded as “still got it”!

Big-boned and convinced he was made for Lycra, he brings humour, determination and enough protection at the front to shelter all of Yorkshire from the wind.

Together, we are an unlikely crew: united by friendship, questionable training plans and the shared belief that snacks count as sports nutrition. We may not look like a cycling team, but we are determined to ride every mile, support one another and avoid embarrassing ourselves too badly.

Behind the banter is a serious commitment to a good cause. Steve knows the training, early starts and inevitable sore backsides are small sacrifices compared with the challenges faced by those the ride supports. Doing it alongside good people, and in Janet’s memory, has made even the toughest days bearable. Making a difference makes every mile worthwhile.

Family first. A promise kept. An unlikely team. And Yorkshire’s most dependable windbreak.
